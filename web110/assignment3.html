The Compare Search Engines activity showed me that different search engines can give different results even when I search for the same thing. I learned that Google, Bing, Yahoo, and other search engines can organize information in different ways. I also realized that the first website that appears is not always the best source. I can use this in college by comparing results and looking at different websites before choosing information for my assignments. The Use a Boolean Search activity was useful because I learned how AND, OR, and NOT can make my searches more specific. These words can help me combine keywords, search for different options, or leave out information that I do not need. I did not think much about how the words I type into a search can change the results. I can use this when researching college projects or looking for information about graphic design, web design, and computer programs. The Evaluate Search Results activity helped me understand that I should be careful about the information I find online. I learned that it is important to check who wrote the information, when it was published, and whether it is accurate. A website being near the top of the results does not always mean it is reliable. I can use this for my college assignments by checking my sources before using them and making sure the information is trustworthy.
