The Social Networking activity helped me understand what social media really is and how different websites are used for different reasons. I learned that social media is not only for talking with friends or posting pictures. It can also be used to share information, communicate with groups, build connections, and even find career opportunities. I also learned that different social media platforms have different purposes, so the best platform depends on what someone is trying to do. I can use this knowledge in my everyday life by choosing the right platform for school, communication, or future work. The LinkedIn Basics activity taught me more about using social media for professional networking. I learned that LinkedIn is different from platforms that are mainly used for entertainment because it focuses more on careers, professional connections, profiles, and resumes. A good profile can show a person's skills, education, and experience to other people or employers. In the future, I can use LinkedIn to build my professional profile, connect with people in graphic design, and look for internships or jobs. The Instagram activity showed me how social media can be used to share pictures and other visual content. I learned that Instagram uses features such as hashtags, links, and profiles to help people organize and share content with others. This was especially interesting to me because I am studying graphic design and will probably work with visual content in the future. I could use Instagram to share some of my design projects, see other creative work, and build an online portfolio that shows my skills. The Honeycomb Model activity helped me understand that social media platforms are not all the same. The model looks at different parts of social media, including identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, and groups. I learned that some platforms are stronger in certain areas than others. This helped me think about why people choose different social media sites depending on their goals. I can apply this in the future by thinking about what I need from a platform before using it, especially for school, communication, or my graphic design career.
