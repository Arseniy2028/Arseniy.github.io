What I Learned

In this assignment, I learned more about graphics and multimedia and how they are used on websites. I learned that raster images are made of pixels, while vector images are made of shapes and lines that can be resized without losing quality. I also learned how to use Inkscape to work with vector graphics and how to add images to a webpage using HTML. Another thing I learned was how audio and video can be added to a website to make it more interesting. Overall, this assignment helped me understand how different types of media work together when creating a website.
